A super quick Sierpiński triangle generator I wrote up in an afternoon while procrastinating with assignments. Simulates a chaos game with a regular n-gon of whatever size you input. Uses matplotlib to plot the fractal (and tkinter since it's so quick to use). 

Not particularly impressive but this method for generating fractals was mentioned in my statistics lecture as a counterexample to the intuition that randomness \(\neq\) patterns. 

Very insignificant but I had fun doing it.
