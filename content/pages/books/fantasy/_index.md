To create a world is a wonderful thing --- I worldbuild myself and I love doing it since it's such a creative exercise, and to hold a small world in your mind and shape it is really fun. Fantasy books are at the core of my inspiration, and I've loved them for the longest time. 

My favourite book in the genre is Christopher Paolini's Inheritance. Choosing the best fantasy book to me is either an impossible question (since I've read so many books I love) or simply 'The Silmarillion' (since, I think that objectively it is *the best* in terms of what fantasy should do, creating an engaging, mystical world)

I don't like that answer as much though, since The Silmarillion is a fantastic book, but it's not as fun to read as A Game of Thrones has been to me, which has been one of the most entertaining books I've picked up in a while. 

That's one of the reasons I love fantasy, the books are usually quite easy[^*] to read compared to texts on mathematics and philosophy, since they're always grounded in our world, rather than a much greater one. 

One thing about fantasy books however, many of them come in really long series (Robert Jordan), which means I've accumulated multiple authors whose works I have started but can't forsee finishing for a while.

[^*]: Parts of A Game of Thrones are quite brutal and I found difficult to get through, but the book is just really good anyway.
