Books about maths can be fun, books *about* maths are usually really dense, difficult to get through, and extremely enlightening. In these I'll try and give a gauge for what level I think these are at, since I've read books I'd consider appropriate to curious non-mathematicians, and those which I'd consider the reader to need some foundational level of confidence in maths beforehand.
