Mathematics and Philosophy have always been linked closely. Think René Descartes (and therefore you am). Not only was the guy a foundational philosopher, but the Cartesian plane is named for him.

And you know what's even better than doing maths and philosophy? That's right- doing ~~maths of philosophy~~ philosophy of maths. In some sense, this is super easy. Just sit and ask yourself 'what exactly is mathematics?' and you're well on your way to some terribly complex thoughts about what really has no right to be any more than a few numbers.
