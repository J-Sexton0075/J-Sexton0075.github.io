> Then I saw when the Lamb broke one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying as with a voice of thunder, "Come!" I looked, and behold, a white horse, and he who sat on it had a bow; and a crown was given to him, and he went out conquering and to conquer.
-- Revelation 6:1-2

Context: The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse are four biblical figures featuring heavily in the highly acclaimed documentary *Good Omens*. The first of the Four Horsemen rides a white horse and one of the theories for what this first horseman could represent is the Antichrist. Charles Freidrich Zimpel was a prussian man who clearly did not like Napoleon. He also appears to have been a railroad builder and a practitioner of various homeopathic medicines. 

I'm yet to confirm whether he actually ever wrote this anywhere and it appears the book he wrote it in costs 16 euros, but this still might be my favourite quote *ever*.
