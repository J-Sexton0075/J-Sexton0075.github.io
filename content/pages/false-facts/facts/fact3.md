# Contrary to popular myth, Liz Truss was not the prime minister with the shortest tenture;
That can be actually be attributed to the Earl of Wrexham, Sir Henry Doble, who was only prime minister for six days in 1809. 
He reportedly lost the job due to a poorly timed joke in the presence of King George III, but very few records survive on the matter.
